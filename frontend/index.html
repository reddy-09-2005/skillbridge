What it does

One upload, a ranked list of roles worth applying to.

SkillBridge AI reads a resume's raw text, identifies the skills in it, and scores that skill set against real job postings. Instead of scanning dozens of listings by hand, you get a shortlist — ranked by fit, with the gaps called out.

How it's used

From resume to shortlist in four steps

01

Upload a resume

PDF, DOCX, or plain text — dropped in from the dashboard.

02

Skills get extracted automatically

No forms to fill in — the text is parsed and key skills are pulled out directly.

03

Add optional filters

Narrow by keyword or location, or let it default to your top detected skills.

04

Review ranked matches

Each result shows a match score and exactly which skills matched or fell short.
